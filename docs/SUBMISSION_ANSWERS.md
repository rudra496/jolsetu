## Short abstract (150 words)
> Bangladesh's groundwater arsenic crisis is the largest poisoning of a population in history:
> 20 million people drink water above the national standard, and arsenic causes 24,000+ adult
> deaths every year. Yet testing happens once in decades, results are human color-chart guesses,
> and families have no way to find a verified safe well. JolSetu closes that loop. Point your
> camera at a $1 Gutzeit test kit and AI reads it instantly; each result is QR-stamped, HMAC-signed
> and GPS-geofenced into a live, tamper-proof well map; a risk model trained on 6,593 real
> measurements finds where the map and model disagree so communities test where it matters; and
> every family gets offline, in-Bangla walking guidance to the nearest verified safe well. Built
> and running today on real data, with 23 passing tests and a transparent model card.

## Impact statement
> Well-switching is the proven, cheapest arsenic intervention (UNICEF delivered it at ≈US$11 per
> person). JolSetu industrializes it: kits people already buy become digital instruments, every test
> strengthens a public map, and the map routes families to safe water the same day. Pilot KPI:
> verified wells tested per union per month; head KPI: person-days of exposure avoided (exposure →
> well-switch → reduced mortality, per Bull WHO 2012 hazard ratios). The same stack covers the
> global arsenic belt — ~94 million people across South and Southeast Asia (Science 2020).

## Innovation statement
> Three firsts vs. the existing landscape (iArsenic = static 3-input screen; Nolkup = government
> registry; Arsenic 4M = new hardware): (1) CV reading of the existing $1 kit — no new hardware,
> validated by peer-reviewed phone-photometry; (2) an active-learning loop where the model sends the
> crowd to test where uncertainty is highest — the map gets smarter with every check-in; (3)
> trust infrastructure (signed codes + geofence + duplicate detection) so an open map can't be
> poisoned by bad data. Fresh UX: photo → instant ppb → spoken Bangla verdict → QR stamp → route.

## Feasibility statement
> The prototype is live and built on 6,593 real, DOI-cited well measurements with 23 automated
> tests. Hardware needed: a $1 kit (already sold in every Bangladeshi bazaar) and any Android
> phone. 8-week plan: 2-school pilot with per-batch chart calibration → server ledger with officer
> review → national model v1 → SMS fallback for feature phones → impact audit.
